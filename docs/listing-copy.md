## 4. Passport America: suggested limits

Passport America's 50% discount applies to the nightly rate only, never weekly or monthly, and
only when a space is open. The park sets its own limits on top of that. The suggested set:

- **Stay cap:** 2 nights at the discount. Any further nights are charged the regular $45.
- **Days:** Sunday through Thursday nights only. No discount on Friday or Saturday nights.
- **Blackouts:** no discount on holiday weekends or big Bandera event weekends.
- **Nightly only:** this is Passport America's own rule, so stating it just avoids arguments.
- **Booking:** by phone or at the office, not through the website. The online booking flow has no
  discount code, so the office applies the discount at check-in after seeing the member card.
- **Doesn't combine** with the military discount.

The cap matters because only a few sites are open to book at a time. On 2026-11-03 most sites
were held by long-term residents. If every free night went at $22.50, the discount would cost
more than the ad is worth. For comparison, the neighbour Serenity Hills RV lists $50 regular and
$25 with Passport America.
