Deposits & cancellations

  • Nightly and weekly reservations require a non-refundable 1-night deposit.
  • Monthly stays require a non-refundable $175 deposit to secure the reservation.
  • Cancel 14+ days before arrival for a refund of amounts paid over the non-refundable deposit.
  • Cancel less than 2 weeks out and you'll receive camping credit (minus the deposit) instead of a refund — credits expire 1 year from the original reservation date and can't be used on holiday/event weekends.
  • Monthly payments are due on or before the 1st; a $15 late fee applies on the 3rd, plus $5/day after that.

Good to know

Site & RV

  • 1 RV, 2 vehicles, 2 adults per RV — 4 guests max per site.
  • Speed limit is 5 mph on all park roads.
  • Quiet hours: 10:00pm – 6:00am.
  • RVs 15+ years old need park approval before booking.

Around the park

  • Smoking permitted at individual sites only — not in common areas.
  • Fires limited to fire pits only.
  • A dumpster is available on site for guest trash.
  • No tents, tarps, pools, or ATVs/side-by-sides on the property.

This is a summary of the park's posted Rules & Guidelines, not the complete list. The full rules are posted at the office and are subject to change at any time.
