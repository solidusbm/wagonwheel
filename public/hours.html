Rates & cancellations

  • No stay costs more than the site's monthly rate, per month. A stay that would otherwise add up to more than a month's rate is charged the monthly rate; longer stays are charged by the month.
  • Electric is not included in the monthly rate. On any booking charged at the monthly rate — including a shorter stay that reached the cap — electric is read at the meter and settled separately at the office. Nightly and weekly rates include electric.
  • Not every site is rented by every term. Some are available by the month only; the booking page shows the rates each site is actually offered at.
  • Cancelling a booking that was charged at the monthly rate — including a shorter stay that reached the cap — carries a $100 service fee. Everything else paid is refunded.
  • Cancelling any other booking carries a service fee of 11.11% of the amount charged. The rest is refunded.

Good to know

Site & RV

  • 1 RV, 2 vehicles, 2 adults per RV — 4 guests max per site.
  • Speed limit is 5 mph on all park roads.
  • Quiet hours: 10:00pm – 6:00am.
  • RVs 15+ years old need park approval before booking.

Around the park

  • Smoking permitted at individual sites only — not in common areas.
  • Fires limited to fire pits only.
  • A dumpster is available on site for guest trash.
  • No tents, tarps, pools, or ATVs/side-by-sides on the property.

This is a summary of the park's posted Rules & Guidelines, not the complete list. The full rules are posted at the office and are subject to change at any time.
