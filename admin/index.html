Photos
Upload photos here — check "Show on homepage" for the ones that should appear in the gallery on the home page. Every uploaded photo (homepage or not) also shows on the public /gallery.html page. Images over 8MB are rejected.

Amenities
One catalog for every amenity. Each one can independently show on the homepage's "What every site includes" grid, be toggleable per site (like "Wired Ethernet"), or both — check any combination. Add, rename, deactivate, or delete, no code change needed.

Content
Edit the homepage and hours-page text directly. Changes apply the next time a visitor loads the page — no code change or redeploy needed.

Everything that can be worked out from the park's own data already is — the headline comes from each page, and the rates and amenities in the structured data come from the Sites and Amenities panels above. Review requests
A short email to a guest a day or two after they leave, asking for a Google review. Reviews are one of the strongest things there is for getting the park found locally. It goes to everyone who stayed and offers nothing in exchange — screening for happy guests or paying for reviews is against Google's rules and gets listings penalised. Nothing sends until this is switched on and a review link is saved.

RoverPass / Hipcamp sync feeds
One iCal feed per site, listing this app's booked dates only (no guest details). Paste a site's URL into RoverPass's or Hipcamp's "import calendar" field once those accounts exist, so a direct booking here blocks the site there too.

Payments
Which Square locations the configured credentials can actually see, and whether the one this site is set to can take card payments. A location can exist, be active, and still not be cleared to process cards — that is what "not authorized to take payments" means at checkout.

Booking alert email
When a guest completes a booking, the park gets an email with the site, dates, guest details and total. The alert address can be a comma-separated list if more than one person should get them. Guests also get their own confirmation with the site, dates, arrival times, the office rule and the cancellation terms — "Preview the guest email" sends you a copy of exactly what they receive, using a fake booking. The sample is a monthly-rate booking on purpose: that version also carries the electric-not-included notice and the flat $100 cancellation wording, so it shows every claim the email can make.
