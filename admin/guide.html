Admin Guide

How to use banderawagonwheelrv.com/admin

This walks through every section of the admin panel in the order it appears on the page, with the everyday tasks for each one. It's written for the office, not for a developer — if a step here doesn't match what you're seeing, the panel may have been reordered since this was written (sections can be moved with the arrows in each heading), so look for the section by name rather than by position.

Reservations

Top of the page

Every upcoming and pending booking, sorted by check-in date.

  • Add a phone-in or walk-in booking: press + New booking, fill in the site, dates, and guest details, and press Save booking. No card is charged from this form — it's for bookings taken outside the website.
  • Edit a booking: press Edit on its row.
  • View or print a guest's application: press Application on the row. From there you can edit any field, and there's a link to open the printable paper version to hand to the guest at check-in.
  • Cancel a booking: press Cancel on the row and confirm. This releases the site for those dates.
  • Refund a guest: if the booking was paid by card, a Refund & cancel button appears on its row. Pressing it refunds the guest through Square and cancels the booking in the same step, so the dates are released right away.

Social posts

Collapsible section

Ready-to-post text for Facebook and Google, written from the park's real, current data — which sites are actually free, the real rates.

  1. Read the draft and edit the text if you want to change anything.
  2. Press Copy text.
  3. Press Open Facebook or Open Google and paste it in.
  4. If a draft has a photo attached, drag it into the post window yourself, or open it full size and save it — there's no automatic attachment.

Nothing posts on its own — every draft has to be copied and pasted by hand. If Facebook's or Google's own posting page ever moves, update the two links at the top of this section and press Save links.

Sites

Collapsible section

Every site's name, rates, and details, including which per-site amenities (from the Amenities catalog below) apply to it.

  • Add a new site: press + New site.
  • Change a site's rate: press Edit on its row, then type the new figure into Price per night, Price per week, or Price per month. If a site isn't rented on a given term, tick that term's N/A box instead of leaving it blank — at least one term has to stay set.
  • Mark a site permanently occupied (a long-term resident, not available to book): set Permanently occupied to Yes in the site editor.
  • Choose a site's photos, and which one guests see first: open the Photos section inside the site editor and tick the ones that apply — the first one you tick is the one guests see on that site's card.
  • Press Save site when you're done.

Photos

Collapsible section

Upload and manage the park's photo library. Every uploaded photo also appears on the public gallery page, whether or not it's flagged for the homepage.

  • Upload a photo: choose a file, optionally add a caption, tick Show on homepage if it should appear there, and press Upload. Files over 8MB are rejected.
  • Reorder photos: use the ← and → arrows on each photo. A photo marked "Home #" shows its position in the homepage gallery specifically.
  • Remove a photo: press Delete on it.

Review requests

Collapsible section

An automatic email asking a guest for a Google review, sent a day or two after they check out.

  • Turn the toggle on, paste the park's Google review link into Your Google review link, set Days to wait after check-out, and press Save. Nothing sends until both the toggle is on and a link is saved.
  • See what a guest gets: press Send me a preview.
  • Send any that are currently due: press Send any that are due now.

It goes to every guest who stayed, with nothing offered in exchange — picking and choosing who gets asked is against Google's rules and risks the listing.

Amenities

Collapsible section

One shared list of amenities used by both the homepage and individual site cards.

  • Add one: type its name and press + Add.
  • Each amenity can be checked to show on the homepage's "what every site includes" grid, to be toggleable per site, or both.
  • Turn one off without deleting it: press Deactivate. Remove it entirely: press Delete.

Content

Collapsible section

Direct editing of the homepage and hours-page text — the hero heading and subtitle, the About section, and the hours-page intro.

Click into any text box, make your change, and click away. A small Saved confirmation appears next to the field. Changes go live the next time someone loads the page — no developer or deploy needed.

Park map

Collapsible section

The map guests see when choosing a site — where every pad, road, and building actually sits, in real feet.

  • Move a pad, road, or building: with the Select tool active, drag it. Drag a corner to resize, or click it and type exact numbers in the panel that appears.
  • Add a pad: + Site. Add a building: + Building. Draw a road: switch to the Draw road tool.
  • Remove something from the map: click it, then Take off the map (or the equivalent remove button). This only removes it from the map — it does not delete the site itself, which is still managed in the Sites section above.
  • Nothing changes for guests until you press Save map. Revert discards anything you haven't saved yet.

Search & sharing

Collapsible section

How the park shows up in a Google search result and in a shared link's preview card. Most of this is already worked out automatically from the Sites and Amenities panels — what's here is the handful of things that need a person's judgment: the Google Search Console verification code, which photo appears on shared links, and the written description for each page's search result. Press Save search settings after changing any of them.

RoverPass / Hipcamp sync feeds

Collapsible section

One calendar feed per site, listing only which dates are booked here (no guest details). Press Copy next to a site and paste that link into RoverPass's or Hipcamp's "import calendar" field, once those accounts exist. That keeps a booking made directly on this site from also being bookable through those platforms.

Booking alert email

Collapsible section

When a guest completes a booking, the park's email address gets a message with the site, dates, guest details, and total. More than one address can receive it — the alert address can hold a comma-separated list.

  • Test that alerts are working: press Send a test email.
  • See exactly what a guest's own confirmation looks like: press Preview the guest email. The sample shown is a monthly-rate booking on purpose, since that version carries every possible line the email can include (the electric-not-included notice and the flat cancellation fee wording).

Push notifications

Collapsible section

Get notified right on this device the moment a guest books, in addition to the email alert. Press Enable on this device to turn it on.

On iPhone or iPad: Safari only delivers notifications to a site that's been added to the Home Screen. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and turn notifications on from the icon you get on the Home Screen rather than from Safari directly.

If something looks wrong

Who to reach depends on what's actually broken:

A guest's card was charged wrong, or a refund needs looking into Handle it in Reservations first (Refund & cancel shows the Square payment ID for that booking); Square's own support can also look up a specific charge directly.
A guest wants to cancel They can use the cancel link in their own confirmation email, or the office can cancel (and refund, if paid) from the Reservations section above.
The site or admin panel won't load, or looks broken Check whether it's just this device first. If it's the whole site, it may be a domain or hosting issue — Cloudflare manages the park's domain and email routing and can help with either.
Something in the app itself is behaving wrong (a form not saving, a page showing the wrong information, anything that looks like a bug rather than a one-off) Contact the developer directly — this isn't something Square or Cloudflare's support can fix.

For anything guest-facing, the park's own listed phone number and email (in the site's footer) are the right place to send a guest who calls in confused.
